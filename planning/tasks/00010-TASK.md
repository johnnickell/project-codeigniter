  Unit-only 100% proposal with Agent OS's all-suite meaningful Domain/Application metric and separate unit
  attribution. Broad adoption approval does not silently choose between them or weaken existing requirements.

  TASK. Distinguish the approved adoption direction from concrete unresolved policy choices and implemented
